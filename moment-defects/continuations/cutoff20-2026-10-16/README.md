## Mathematical statement and scope

For the explicitly retained analytic and historical certificate premises A,
the completed finite continuation gives

$$A\implies C_n<C_{\mathrm E}\quad\hbox{for every integer }n\ge20.$$

There is a complete actual-index base at 20, followed by four separate
implications from a **global** sharp predecessor to the sharp conclusion at
21, 22, 23 and 24. The retained 25--30 continuation and the tail from 31 then
supply the rest. No monotonicity of the sequence C_n is assumed.

Each actual index covers the closed third-moment bands [1,11/10] and
[11/10,9/8], all 100 closed probability panels, and the high-moment region
[9/8,infinity). All restrictions concern the same unrestricted attained
contrary maximizer. A moment-restricted optimizer is not substituted for it.

The selected low-moment count covers contain 144,020 leaves in total:

| n | First band | Second band | Total |
|---|-----------:|------------:|------:|
| 20 | 37,842 | 50,144 | 87,986 |
| 21 | 9,402 | 12,555 | 21,957 |
| 22 | 6,748 | 7,694 | 14,442 |
| 23 | 5,751 | 5,322 | 11,073 |
| 24 | 4,714 | 3,848 | 8,562 |

These numbers do not replace the separate support, resource, Fourier and
analytic implications. Some index-20 material is retained from earlier work.

**This is an incremental, conditional certificate package, not a standalone
unconditional proof or a portable whole-DAG replay.** The original status
explicitly leaves `unqualified_cutoff20_proved` false. Inherited generic
analytic implications and historical computational ancestors remain retained
premises. The archive reports 186 unresolved names for the base at 20 and
18,719 unresolved paths in a finite location test for the older tail. These
overlapping counters are not added, and unresolved does not mean absent
everywhere. See the original reports copied alongside the archive.

The manuscript's independent presentation of the effective argument from
31 is retained. The extension to 20 is stated as a separate conditional
theorem; it is not a cutoff for two-point optimality.

## Integrity inspection, not mathematical verification

With ordinary Python 3.11 or newer, run:

```sh
python -B -S verify_archive.py
```

This lightweight, standard-library-only inspector checks the complete ZIP
hash and three pinned entries, then reads the small final status. It streams
the large certificate rather than loading it into memory. It does **not**
extract or execute archived code, rerun any count leaf, reconstruct missing
ancestors, or verify the mathematical result. A successful integrity check
does not change any conditional or false verification flag.
